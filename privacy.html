Privacy Policy

This application is a personal tool that accesses your Google Drive files to sync a local Obsidian vault between devices using rclone.

What we access: files and folders under the configured Drive folder ("SecondBrain") needed for bidirectional sync. We do not access other Google services beyond what is required for Drive access.

How data is used: only to read and write files on your local machine and in your own Google Drive account for the purpose of synchronization. No data is transmitted to third parties other than Google Drive during sync, and no data is logged to external services by this consent app.

Storage: local copies remain on the devices you authorize. Credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored locally in your rclone configuration on those devices.
